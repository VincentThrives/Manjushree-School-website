Why Sport Matters

Fitness, Teamwork, Resilience

Sport at Manjushree is not just about winning trophies. Physical education is a regular part of the timetable, and every child takes part — running, playing and learning how to work as a team.

We believe that the habits built on the playground — fair play, patience, turning up on time — are as important as the ones built in the classroom.

  • Weekly PE periods for every class
  • Annual sports day with inter-house competitions
  • Participation in taluk-level and school-cluster events

A Big Day for Everyone

Annual Sports Day

Once a year the school turns into a stadium. Inter-house competitions, march past, races for every class and prize distribution — a day parents and children look forward to.

  • March past and school assembly
  • Track events for every age group
  • Inter-house team games
  • Prize distribution and closing ceremony
Sport teaches what a textbook cannot — how to lose gracefully, win humbly, and try again tomorrow.

Those are the habits we hope every Manjushree student carries beyond the school gate.
