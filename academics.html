Academics at Manjushree

An English medium programme built around the Karnataka State Board syllabus — with room for children to grow academically, socially and personally.

What We Teach

A Continuous Path from LKG to Class 10

Structured, age-appropriate learning at every stage.

Pre-PrimaryLKG and UKG — early literacy, numeracy and school routines through play and activity.
PrimaryClasses 1 to 5 — building strong basics in English, Kannada, mathematics, EVS and general knowledge.
Middle SchoolClasses 6 to 8 — introducing subject specialisation, scientific thinking and independent study habits.
SecondaryClasses 9 and 10 — focused preparation for the Karnataka SSLC examination.
A+Learner-centred, values-based English medium education
How We Teach

Regular Practice. Real Understanding.

Our classrooms are calm and structured. Teachers explain in English, but use Kannada when it helps a child understand — because comprehension comes first, and confidence in English grows steadily with practice.

We track how each student is doing through regular class work, unit tests and term examinations, and share honest reports with parents so that home and school can work together.

  • Unit tests and continuous assessment
  • Extra help for students who need to catch up
  • Reading, writing and speaking practice built into the week
  • Regular parent-teacher meetings

Character Matters

Learning to Be Good, As Well As Clever

We spend as much time on how children treat each other as we do on how they read, write and calculate — because good habits, formed young, last a lifetime.

Honesty, respect for elders, care for the classmate next to you, and pride in a job done well — these are the values we try to build every day.

Assembly, story time and small acts of responsibility around the school all help children understand that character is built in the small moments.

An Education for the Whole Child

Strong academics, honest character, healthy activity — together in one school.
