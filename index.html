Welcome to Manjushree

A Caring School in the Heart of Sira

Established in 1994-95, Manjushree English School is a private, co-educational institution serving the families of Keshav Nagar, Balaji Nagar Road and the wider Sira community — offering English medium education aligned with the Karnataka State Board curriculum.

  • Co-educational classrooms — girls and boys learn together
  • Karnataka State Board syllabus, taught in English
  • Continuous education from the primary years through Class 10

What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

🌟

Our Vision

To be a trusted neighbourhood school where every child grows into a confident learner, a responsible citizen and a lifelong seeker of knowledge — rooted in local values and open to the wider world.

🎯

Our Mission

To offer affordable, quality English medium education that combines academic rigour with character formation — helping each student build strong foundations in language, mathematics, science and social understanding.
