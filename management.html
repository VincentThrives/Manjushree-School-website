The People Behind the School

Manjushree English School is run as a private, co-educational institution under the Manjushree Vidya Samsthe — founded in 1994-95 by the late Sri P. M. Ranganathappa.

Founder & Chairman

Sri P. M. Ranganathappa

ಪಿ.ಎಂ. ರಂಗನಾಥಪ್ಪನವರು

Founder — Manjushree Vidya Samsthe

Former MLA, Sira Constituency · ಮಾಜಿ ಶಾಸಕರು, ಶಿರಾ ಕ್ಷೇತ್ರ

12 October 1938 12 February 2013

Sri P. M. Ranganathappa established Manjushree Vidya Samsthe in 1994-95 with the aim of bringing quality English medium education within reach of every family in Sira.

A former Member of the Legislative Assembly for the Sira constituency, he devoted his life to public service and the educational upliftment of his hometown. The school he founded continues to serve the children of Keshav Nagar and beyond in his memory.

How We Work

Principles That Guide Us

A small school, run simply and honestly.

🕊️

Openness

Parents can approach any teacher or the office at any time — no barriers, no bureaucracy.

📘

Focus on Basics

Fairness
