How We Work

Principles That Guide Us

A small school, run simply and honestly.

🕊️

Openness

Parents can approach any teacher or the office at any time — no barriers, no bureaucracy.

📘

Focus on Basics

Reading, writing, mathematics and character — these are the things we insist on getting right.

🤲

Fairness

Every child, regardless of background, gets the same attention and the same expectations.

💰

Affordable

We keep the school within reach of the families we serve — Sira families come first.
