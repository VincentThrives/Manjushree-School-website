For Families

Parent-Teacher Meetings

After each major assessment, parents meet class teachers to review progress, ask questions and agree on the next steps. These meetings are one of the most important events on our calendar.

  • Held twice a term for each class
  • One-on-one conversation with the class teacher
  • Written progress report shared and discussed
The best events on our calendar are the quiet ones — the parent-teacher meeting, the classroom assembly, the small win worth talking about at home.

Those are the moments that build a school over three decades.
