Our Teachers

The Heart of the School

A school is only as good as its teachers. Manjushree is fortunate to have staff who know their students, respect their parents, and take their subject seriously.

🍎

Class Teachers

One teacher who knows every child in the class personally — the first point of contact for parents.

🧑‍🏫

Subject Teachers

Specialist teachers for English, Kannada, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies and other subjects.

PE & Activity Staff

Teachers meet regularly to compare notes on how classes are going, share what is working and plan the year together. A school does its best work when the staff room and the classroom are one team.
